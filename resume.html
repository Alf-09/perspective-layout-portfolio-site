Professional Narrative

Core Identity

After two decades of shaping physical brand ecosystems for corporate clients, I have transitioned into digital product engineering. By blending the visual discipline of a senior graphic designer with the logic of a front end developer, I construct scalable design systems that maintain strict brand integrity.

I thrive at the intersection of aesthetics and code. Viewing product design through a "production first" lens, I leverage AI workflows like Google Gemini to rapidly accelerate front end scaffolding. This hybrid approach allows me to optimize developer handoffs and ensure complex visual concepts translate flawlessly into digital solutions.

Education & Evolution

My foundation in visual architecture was established at RMIT and hardened through 20 years of managing production pipelines at GPI Corporate and Bluegum. I understand exactly how to organize and document creative assets for flawless execution, whether for physical print or digital compilation.

To connect this physical expertise with digital engineering, I formalized my technical stack through the Google UX Design Certificate, Microsoft's Front End Developer Program, and FreeCodeCamp. This hybrid education empowers me to collaborate seamlessly with both creative directors and engineering teams.
